# Wardy demo video (90 seconds)

Film on your Seeker with the built-in screen recorder (swipe down twice → Screen record, with microphone on). Talk calmly, like showing a friend. One take per row is fine; cut them together in CapCut or iMovie.

**Before filming**
- Refresh https://seeker-shield.vercel.app so you have the latest version.
- Optional but strong: run `scripts/demo-setup.ts` so your wallet has a fake "FREE SKR - claim…" token and a test permission for Wardy to catch (see the README).
- Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notifications pop up.

| Time | On screen | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Your face, or the Seeker in your hand | "My Seeker keeps my keys safe in hardware. But drainers don't need my keys. They trick me into approving access." |
| 0:08–0:20 | Open Wardy. The intro plays: Wardy patrols, eats, gets a chest | "This is Wardy. He guards my wallet." |
| 0:20–0:35 | Tap **Scan my wallet for free**, approve in Seed Vault, the score appears | "One tap, and he checks every token I hold, live, in under a second." |
| 0:35–0:50 | Point at a finding: the fake "FREE SKR" token or the test permission. Open **Details** | "Here's a fake airdrop trying to lure me to a scam site, and an app that can still move my tokens. Plain English, no jargon." |
| 0:50–1:05 | Tap **Fix**, review sheet, fingerprint, score jumps, Wardy chomps, "Wardy found you $…" | "One fingerprint. Access removed, and the SOL stuck in old accounts comes back to me." |
| 1:05–1:20 | Unlock Wardy (test mode), tap him, rub him (hearts), show "Fed with today's scan · chest in 6 days" | "Unlocking Wardy means he checks for me. Every scan feeds him, and every 7 days there's a chest." |
| 1:20–1:30 | The Watch tab (Telegram), then the closing screen with Wardy | "He watches my friends' wallets too, and messages me when something lands. Wardy: your keys were already safe. Now everything else is." |

**Tips**
- Keep the phone steady; record the screen, not the phone with another camera.
- If something fails on camera, keep going and re-record just that row.
- Upload to YouTube as "Unlisted" and put the link in the submission and the README.
